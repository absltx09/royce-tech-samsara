We study what stays.

Royce Technologies Samsara is Samsara's oldest research company. For 178 years, we have measured the trace that strong emotion leaves on places and things.

Affective persistence

An object someone is still grieving behaves differently from an object that has been forgotten. Measurably differently.

Our instruments measure that trace. We call it residue.
