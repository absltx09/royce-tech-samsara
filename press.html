2 September 2023

Leo Royce V to lead Royce while his father recovers

Following the sudden illness of our chairman, Leo Royce IV, the board has named his son, Leo Royce V, chief executive, on the recommendation of our founding partner.

"My father built this company to help people hold on to what matters," he said. "Nothing about that changes. If anything, we'll do it faster."

29 August 2023

A statement on our chairman's health

Leo Royce IV was taken ill yesterday evening while working late in the founding archive, and has been admitted to Samsara District General. He is stable and resting. The family thanks everyone for their kindness and asks for privacy.
